# Academic-research figure captions

These are alternative design-study figures. Figure numbers below indicate their order within this package; the manuscript and its existing figures have not been changed.

**Figure 1**

*Proposed Waterfall control through scoped state and candidate-aware decisions.*

Note. An explicit difficulty correction and feedback on displayed items inform bounded, validated state edits. The illustrated edit changes the active task to beginner level while retaining the persistent photography interest. The same accepted state reaches acquisition and ranking. A single controller checks candidate sufficiency and remaining budget before requesting a bounded acquisition batch, ranking available candidates, or holding the current state and order. The pool-status return supports reassessment after acquisition. Model parameters remain frozen. Dashed module outlines identify proposed extensions; solid outlines identify component types already represented in the prototype, not a validated end-to-end implementation. Letters are illustrative item identifiers, not relevance grades. This concept figure presents no measured outcomes.

**Figure 2**

*A planned paired, multi-round comparison under matched starting conditions.*

Note. Both methods receive the same permitted initial history, sources, frozen model, tool access, scoring backend, and budget. They maintain independent state and candidate pools and can display different slates. The illustrated correction is released to both methods before Round 2; future instructions remain hidden until their scheduled release. Within each lane, feedback concerns only that method's displayed items and updates its next state. Feedback generated by a simulator is synthetic. An independent, method-blind judge assesses usefulness, with human audit; its labels never feed either policy. Full-trajectory slates and logs support three measurement groups: useful distinct items counted once per duplicate group and ranking quality; exposures required after a correction and inappropriate changes to persistent state; and source calls, tokens, and update latency. Resource costs are read from execution logs, not inferred by the usefulness judge. The judge is separate from the feedback generator. Letters are illustrative identifiers and three rounds illustrate the protocol, not observed outcomes or a fixed final horizon.
